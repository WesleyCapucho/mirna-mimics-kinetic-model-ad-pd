## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First version of this repository. The mechanistic scripts (`scripts/12`, `13`, `17` to `20` and `36` to `40`), their helpers, the parameter table, the raw kinetic and structure files, the result tables and the figures were moved here from an earlier repository of the same author (10.5281/zenodo.23438256), where they were archived up to its version 1.2.1. They were copied unchanged except for two docstrings that cited scripts of that repository, and `scripts/08` was reduced to the checks of this study (783 checks). `scripts/46` is new: it ranks the nine mimic series by the longest dosing interval within 3, 10 or 30 times the baseline pool, at every anchor of the repression strength, and records whether first place depends on those choices; `scripts/08` recomputes the ranking. `scripts/47` draws the figure of the ranking, with the renders of the targets above the plots of the longest dosing interval over the scan of the repression strength (main-text figure). Two defects of the figures inherited from the earlier repository were fixed: the lettering of seven figures was below the 4.5:1 contrast that the journal asks for (lowest 2.26:1), and the renders were embedded at 100 dpi in the PDF and EPS files (600 dpi in the PNG), because the vector backends resample an image to the figure resolution. `scripts/45` measures both on the vector files and `scripts/08` keeps them in check (1045 checks). `scripts/48` is new: a two-part scheme of the molecular mechanism of the two diseases and of the six steps of the algorithm (reading the table, closed form, repeated dose, dose solution, sweep and ranking, checks), in English and Brazilian Portuguese. `scripts/49` and `scripts/50` are new: the sequence-level pairing of miR-29a/b/c-3p with the 3′UTR of BACE1 (RefSeq NM_012104.6, 3,868 nt) and of miR-7-5p and miR-153-3p with the 3′UTR of SNCA (NM_000345.4, 2,529 nt), both MANE Select records taken from NCBI, with the mature sequences from miRBase, archived with their SHA-256 (`data/raw/sequences_2026/`) and cross-checked against UCSC refGene and hg38 (SNCA identical; the BACE1 refGene 3′UTR is the RefSeq one plus 12 nt beyond every site), and the figure of the sites opened as duplexes; `scripts/08` repeats the scan with a separate loop. Before this version was archived, the figures and the numbers were revised: the intervals quoted in the text are now rounded to the nearest value, as in the figures (they were rounded down); the one combination in which first place is tied (all series at 0 h) is counted apart, so that first place is held by the same series in 32 of the 33 combinations and shared in the other; the renders of the ranking figure are larger and aligned with their columns, and the contour labels, legends and axes of four other figures were tidied.
